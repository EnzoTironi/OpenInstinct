# Community conduct

Keep Zoen's issues, pull requests and project spaces useful and welcoming. Treat
people with respect, discuss ideas on their merits, and make criticism specific
and constructive. Give others room to learn and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, impersonation, publishing private
information, spam and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not bring
customer conversations or credentials into public discussions.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict
participation. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so does not
expose private information or put someone at risk.

Report community concerns to a repository maintainer through an available private
contact channel. Do not repeat private details in public reports. Maintainers
should handle reports discreetly and consider affected participants' safety.
Product vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
